## Content (HTML)

<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The North of Grand Neighborhood will hold its Annual Meeting Tuesday, Nov. 12, 2013, beginning at 7 p.m. at Grand Suites, 3801 Grand Avenue.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Ted Irvine, owner of The Mansion and member of Des Moines Plan &amp; Zoning, will be the guest speaker. Ted will give us an update of the Ingersoll-Grand Commercial Urban Renewal Plan.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Also, please join us to cast your vote for 2014 board members. These seats are open:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">President</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Vice President</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Treasurer</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">At-Large (3 positions)</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">If you are interested in serving on the board of directors, please contact board member Matt Coen, <a href="mailto:mcoen@realizeyourvision.net" target="_blank"><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt; color: #0000ff;">mcoen@realizeyourvision.net</span></a>. Nominations will also be accepted at the meeting. All terms are two years and begin Jan. 1, 2014.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">It is with gratitude that we recognized the long-serving, out-going North of Grand board members who have made a tremendous difference in organizing and promoting our events in the neighborhood related to crime prevention, BridgePark, beautification / clean up / SCRUB, garage sales, annual picnic, historical research, economic development, Ingersoll Ave. improvements and fund raising: <strong><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Kimberly Hansen, Ben Molloy, Mark Siegfried, Matt Braafhart, Matt Coen</span></strong> and <strong><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Nathan DeCock</span></strong>. Thank you to them for dedicating their time for the betterment of our neighborhood!</span></p>
