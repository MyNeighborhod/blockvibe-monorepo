# Sign Up Now for 2013 Neighborhood Garage Sale

- Slug: sign-up-now-for-2013-neighborhood-garage-sale

## Content (HTML)

<p><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">The North of Grand Neighborhood will hold its annual Neighborhood Garage Sale June 7-8, 2013. This is a fun, annual event that helps you make a little money on your old, unused stuff that someone else may be dying to have!</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">There is NO COST for current dues-paying members of the NOG Neighborhood Association to participate. <em><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">You may register now and pay dues in order to participate</span></em>. See the &#8220;Membership&#8221; page of this Web site for information or contact NOG President Kimberly Hansen, (515) 279-2684 or <span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;"><a href="mailto:kimberlymhansen@aol.com"><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt; color: #0000ff;">kimberlymhansen@aol.com</span></a> regarding membership.</span></span></p>
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">THE DEADLINE TO REGISTER IS JUNE 3, 2013.</span></span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">A list of participating neighborhood sales will be promoted via:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">Craigslist.org;</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">The Des Moines Register classifieds,</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">The North of Grand Facebook Page, Facebook.com/north.of.grand.dsm;</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">This Web site; and</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">The email list of current NOG members.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-family: arial; font-size: 10pt;">On June 6 or 7, participants will receive a yard sign and maps to hand out that mark other garage sale locations, hours of operation and a brief description of items.</span></p>
