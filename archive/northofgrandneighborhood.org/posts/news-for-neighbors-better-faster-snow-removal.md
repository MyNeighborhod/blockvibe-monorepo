<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;"><a href="http://northofgrandneighborhood.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/snowplow.jpg"><img decoding="async" loading="lazy" class="alignright size-full wp-image-377" title="snowplow" src="http://northofgrandneighborhood.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/snowplow.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="225" /></a>New for the 2012/2013 snow season all residential streets will be plowed after 2 inch snowfalls.  Streets will be cleared with larger snow plows known as “wing-plows.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Wing plows can clear a path 14 feet wide.  This larger path requires vehicles to be off the street to make the equipment as efficient as possible.  <em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Therefore parking will be restricted until residential streets are plowed curb to curb.</span></em></span></p>
