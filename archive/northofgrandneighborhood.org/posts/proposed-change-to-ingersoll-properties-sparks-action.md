# Proposed Change to Ingersoll Properties Sparks Action

- ID: 585
- Slug: proposed-change-to-ingersoll-properties-sparks-action
- Date: 2013-10-21T11:45:39
- Original URL: https://northofgrandneighborhood.org/?p=585

## Content (HTML)

<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The Oct. 17 meeting of the DM Plan &amp; Zoning Commission included this on its agenda:</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">“Request from Lee Family Properties LLC (owner) represented by Andy Lee (officer) for review and approval of a Site Plan under design guidelines in NPC Districts on property located at 3701 Ingersoll Avenue, to allow revision to off-street parking configuration and landscaping.”</span></p>
<p><a href="http://northofgrandneighborhood.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ITEM-7.pdf" target="_blank"><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt; color: #0000ff;">See the proposed site plan</span></a></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">In response, the North of Grand Board of Directors drafted the following letter, which was read before P&amp;Z Oct. 17:</span></p>
<p><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The Board of Directors for the North of Grand Neighborhood Association has reviewed the site plan as well as the City staff recommendations for the properties located at 3701-3711 Ingersoll Ave.  Pursuant to our review and discussion, we offer the following comments:</span></em></p>
<p><em><strong><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">WE AGREE / SUPPORT </span></strong></em></p>
<ul>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Streetscaping as proposed by City Staff.</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Adherence to NPC guidelines.</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">That the project be contingent on timeline / deadline and performance bonding.</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The removal of the pole/sign bases at SE and SW corners of site.</span></em></li>
<p><em><em> </em></em><em><em> </em></em><em><em> </em></em></ul>
<p><em><em> </em><em><em><strong><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">WE DON&#8217;T AGREE / DON&#8217;T SUPPORT</span></strong></em></em></em></p>
<ul>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The proposed 6&#8242; easement on residential property to north in lieu of 10’ parking setback.</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Any removal of trees on the adjacent property to the north.</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">4 on-street parking spaces on west side of 37th Street (currently no parking).</span></em></li>
<li><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Any reduction in minimum parking requirements.</span></em></li>
<p><em><em> </em></em><em><em> </em></em></ul>
<p><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Numerous infractions and unfulfilled agreements have been associated with these properties over the last months and years.  In this case, the Neighborhood believes past behavior is a reasonable indicator of future behavior.  We have little confidence that the site plan requirements will be completed in a timely and satisfactory manner, if at all.</span></em></p>
<p><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Therefore, it is the position of our board, that the proposed site plan meet, or exceed, all minimum requirements, as well as the requirements recommended by City Staff.</span></em></p>
<p><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Sincerely,</span></em></p>
<p><em> </em><em> </em><em><em><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">Kimberly Hansen, President<br />
<span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">North of Grand Neighborhood Association</span></span></em></em></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The Commission deferred the item until its Nov. 7 meeting, which will begin at 5:30 p.m. in City Council Chambers, 400 Robert D. Ray Drive. You can view the agenda and meeting minutes here: <a href="http://www.dmgov.org/government/boards/PlanandZoningCommission/Pages/Meetings.aspx" target="_blank"><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt; color: #0000ff;">http://www.dmgov.org/government/boards/PlanandZoningCommission/Pages/Meetings.aspx</span></a>.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">We encourage any and all Ingersoll business owners and residents who wish to speak to this issue to please plan to attend the Nov. 7 meeting and voice your opinion.</span></p>
