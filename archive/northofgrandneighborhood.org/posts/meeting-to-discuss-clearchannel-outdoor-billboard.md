<p><span style="font-family: Arial; font-size: 10pt;">The North of Grand Neighborhood Association invites residents to a special meeting regarding the proposed new electronic billboard and its location close to I-235 and 42nd Street. If you would like to hear more about the electronic billboard and its proposed location, please plan to attend – your input is important. This item is on the Planning and Zoning Agenda for Thursday Dec. 5, 6 pm.</span></p>
